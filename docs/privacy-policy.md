# Boxer OS Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026
Developer: Praneeth Reddy (prdy)
Contact: team.edyma@gmail.com

## The short version

Boxer OS is a local-first training app. It has no account, no server and no analytics.
Everything you enter or record stays on your phone. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share
any personal data.

## What the app stores on your device

- Your settings: name (optional), stance, level, sound, haptics, voice, keep-screen-on and
  reminder preferences.
- Your training history: sessions you finish, ratings and notes you write, reaction-drill
  results, saved combinations.

All of this lives in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded. Deleting the
app, or using "Clear history" in Settings, removes it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not create accounts or ask you to sign in.
- It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.
- It does not send any data over the network. The app works fully offline.

## Permissions

- Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): only if you turn on reminders in Settings. Reminders
  are scheduled locally on the device; no notification service outside your phone is involved.
- Boot completed (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`): so scheduled reminders survive a restart.
- Vibration and keeping the screen awake during a session are used for training cues.
- Set wallpaper (`SET_WALLPAPER`): only when you choose a poster in Gym Wall and tap set. The
  image is drawn on your device; nothing is downloaded or uploaded.
- Live wallpaper (Gym Wall Live): if you turn it on, the app stores the posters you chose in its
  private storage and Android shows the next one when the screen turns on. Nothing leaves the
  device.
- The app can bind to your phone's text-to-speech engine to speak cues. Speech is generated on
  the device by that engine.

## External links

The About screen contains links to the source code, the license text, this policy and photo
sources. Opening a link launches your browser, which is governed by that site's own policy.

## Children

Boxer OS is a fitness app for people who train boxing. It is not directed at children under 13
and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published at the same address and the effective
date above is updated. Because the app collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.
